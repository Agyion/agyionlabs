# Distinct instrument workspaces

The user requested publication of the pending changes and a further visual revision. The current weakness is structural: most instruments repeat a large identity heading followed by a form on the left and a diagram on the right. Changing accent colors does not make their different jobs easier to recognize.

## Direction

The user's subsequent feedback supersedes the first composition's typography and density. Remove duplicate captions, draft badges, repeated example totals and intro paragraphs; do not solve crowding by reducing font size. Use a centered primary heading, value and action, distinct field groups and generous separation. Visible interface labels and metadata use at least 14 pixels; ordinary explanatory text uses 16 pixels. Optional detail belongs behind a named disclosure. A roughly 62/38 main/support proportion is a layout choice where two regions are useful, not a claim that a mathematical ratio guarantees usability.

Retain the existing Agyion identity, black and warm orange palette, typefaces, interactive space scene, slower orbit controls with the requested 50 percent increase from their preceding setting and remembered flight preference. Group titles, actions and the object being edited inside clear surfaces. Use familiar transaction objects and visual hierarchy instead of additional marketing copy or decorative status indicators.

The catalog should answer what a person can do before asking them to learn the instrument names. Its six objects have different proportions and silhouettes. Detail pages bring the existing working example forward, retain one example instance and keep conditions available without repeating a second block of introductory copy.

| Instrument | Primary object | App working layout |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fade | Price ticket and falling curve | Price terms read together, with funding and handoff controls below |
| Pod | A timed savings vault | Amount first, then time and explicit key backup |
| Trigger | A payment agreement | Beneficiary, reviewer and expiry form a readable condition sequence |
| Envoy | A limited authority credential | Agent permission and effective claim limits stay primary |
| Ramp | Currency transfer | A compact transfer widget with secondary account details |
| Ledger | Receipts | A wide activity desk with readable record details |

The app's common shell remains consistent so switching tools does not move basic navigation or closing controls. A 94 percent opaque common window separates forms from the bright disk; text, inputs and task cards retain their reading contrast, and the dock becomes one contained navigation group. No background blur pass is introduced. Each instrument owns its interior hierarchy.

## Reference and reasoning

[Nielsen Norman Group's progressive disclosure guidance](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/) supports keeping common actions visible and moving secondary material behind clearly named controls. Applied here: technical explanations remain accessible, while amount, destination, conditions and key safety stay in the working area. Important financial or key-loss conditions must not disappear for visual simplicity.

[Stripe's Checkout product examples](https://stripe.com/payments/checkout) present the purchase amount and payment action as one focused transaction surface. The useful reference here is that hierarchy, not Stripe's brand or a copied template. The layout choices above are design judgments for this project; no user study or conversion improvement is claimed.

## Behavior to preserve and verify

- Existing field labels, validation, signing boundaries, write gate and wallet/secret lifecycle.
- Record loading, feedback, draft preservation across switching, Escape and keyboard tab navigation.
- Six everyday examples, all four stages, failure cases and resets; no simulated payment presented as a real transaction.
- Every launch target, its query parameters, skip preference and continuous cross-document flight.
- No new green accent, em dash prose, fake balances, fake activity, external tracking or dependencies.
- Desktop, tablet and narrow phone layout, scrolling, actual hit targets and readable controls in normal and reduced motion.

The app gas moves 25 percent faster on a separate integrated clock. Both gas layers use the same phase, and a validated optional phase in the flight handoff preserves the first app frame. Camera motion, spacecraft motion and landing gas rate remain unchanged.

Publication follows fresh builds, assembled-page checks, an artifact manifest and live version/asset/UI verification. This task does not deploy contracts or claim a whole-repository security audit.

## Final directed refinements

The orange connector between Fade's price inputs is centered by its rendered glyph across the price-field column, not inferred from heading alignment. Instrument selection follows real rotating module positions from one common side facing the black hole. It no longer uses the bay's rotating outward vector to choose a new viewing hemisphere. Selection does not rotate the ship, and manual orbit remains unrestricted after leaving module focus.
